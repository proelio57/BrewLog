iOS

Android
• The selected recipe is always shown at the top of the Brew screen; tap it to change the recipe
• Choosing a recipe for another brewer (e.g. a V60 recipe while the Switch is selected) selects that brewer
• Espresso: with the scale connected to the app, BrewLog stops the shot on the machine (GaggiMate, Decent) at the target yield in every phase of the profile, and asks again if the pump keeps running
• After a shot BrewLog offers to switch GaggiMate to steam; Settings → After the shot: Ask, Switch to steam or Off
• Pressure view: with a pressure sensor a big colour-coded pressure number and a pressure graph with a target band; with the machine's own pressure weight and pressure side by side (Settings → Live shot: Automatic, Always, Off)
• Landscape full-screen chart with a button or by turning the phone (can be turned off in Settings)
• Bigger, coloured flow (g/s) readout, and the live charts always fit on the screen
